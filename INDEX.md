## Quick Reference: Technique Selection Guide

### For Acute Joint Pain
1. [Mulligan MWM](./manual-therapy/mulligan-concept.md) - First choice for immediate pain relief
2. [McKenzie Method](./manual-therapy/mckenzie-method.md) - If directional preference present
3. [ANF Therapy](./therapeutic-modalities/anf-therapy.md) - For inflammation management

### For Chronic Pain
1. [Pain Neuroscience Education](./pain-science/pain-neuroscience-education.md) - Foundation
2. [Biopsychosocial Approach](./pain-science/biopsychosocial-approach.md) - Comprehensive management
3. [Dry Needling](./fascial-approaches/dry-needling-trigger-points.md) - For myofascial component

### For Movement Dysfunction
1. [Functional Movement Assessment](./neuromuscular-movement/functional-movement.md) - Assessment
2. [Motor Control Training](./neuromuscular-movement/motor-control.md) - Retraining
3. [Corrective Exercise](./therapeutic-modalities/corrective-exercise.md) - Programming

1. [PNF](./neuromuscular-movement/proprioceptive-neuromuscular-facilitation.md) - Core technique
2. [Neurological Rehabilitation](./specialized-rehab/neurological-rehabilitation.md) - Comprehensive approach

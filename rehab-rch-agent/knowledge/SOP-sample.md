# Rehabilitation Department — SOP (Sample)

> Replace this sample with your approved SOP.pdf / Department Guideline.docx.
> Operational content only — no patient information.

## 1. Equipment Request Process
1. Staff completes the Equipment Request Form (description, quantity, justification, urgency).
2. Unit supervisor verifies need and signs.
3. Request is submitted to the Rehabilitation Section Head for approval.
4. Approved requests go to Biomedical / Supply Chain per hospital policy.
5. Requester tracks status weekly; urgent clinical-safety items are escalated same-day.

Responsible roles: Requesting therapist → Unit Supervisor → Section Head → Biomedical/Supply.
Escalation: if no response in 5 working days, escalate to Section Head; safety-critical items escalate immediately.

## 2. Escalation Path
1. Treating team / shift in-charge
2. Unit Supervisor
3. Rehabilitation Section Head
4. Hospital operations / on-call administrator (after hours, per policy)

After-hours urgent operational issues: contact the on-call supervisor, then the Section Head.

## 3. Annual Leave Coverage
1. Leave requests submitted at least 14 days in advance via the approved form.
2. Coverage plan must name the covering therapist and handover notes (operational only, no PHI in chat tools).
3. Unit Supervisor confirms coverage, then Section Head approves.
4. Approved leave roster is posted weekly; swaps require supervisor approval.

## 4. Meeting Cadence
- Weekly staff huddle: Tuesdays 09:00, Rehab conference room.
- Monthly operations review: first Sunday of each month.
- Minutes stored in Google Drive: Rehab RCH Agent / Meeting Minutes.

## 5. Announcements
- All department-wide announcements are drafted via the Rehab RCH Agent and approved by the Section Head before posting.
- Archive: Google Drive / Announcements.

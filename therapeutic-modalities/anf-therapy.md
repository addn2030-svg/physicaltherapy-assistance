Developed by Dr. Mikel H-G Hoff, ANF Therapy aims to reduce inflammation, optimize neurological function, and restore normal physiological frequencies in the body.

## ANF Disc Types and Applications

### Anti-Inflammatory Discs
- **Purpose**: Reduce acute and chronic inflammation
- **Placement**: Over inflamed tissues, joints, or organs
- **Indications**: Acute injuries, chronic inflammatory conditions
- **Duration**: Typically 72 hours per application

### Nerve Discs (Positive and Negative)
- **Purpose**: Optimize nerve function and signal transmission
- **Placement**: Along nerve pathways, over nerve roots, or peripheral nerves

## Assessment and Treatment Protocol

### Initial Assessment
1. **Comprehensive History**: Symptoms, onset, aggravating factors
2. **Physical Examination**: Movement, palpation, functional testing
3. **Inflammation Scanning**: Palpation for heat, swelling, tissue changes
4. **Nerve Pathway Assessment**: Identify nerve involvement and pathways
5. **Treatment Planning**: Determine disc selection and placement strategy

### Treatment Protocol

#### Acute Conditions (0-6 weeks)
1. **Anti-inflammatory Protocol**: Primary focus on reducing inflammation
2. **Nerve Support**: Address pain and nerve dysfunction
3. **Frequency**: Change discs every 72 hours initially
4. **Duration**: 2-4 weeks typically

#### Chronic Conditions (>6 weeks)
1. **Multi-System Approach**: Address inflammation, nerve, and metabolic factors
2. **Layered Protocol**: Multiple disc types for comprehensive treatment
3. **Frequency**: Change discs every 3-5 days
4. **Duration**: 4-12 weeks or longer for complex conditions

### Disc Placement Strategies

#### Local Placement
- Directly over area of pain or dysfunction
- Surrounding inflamed tissues
- Over specific anatomical structures

#### Nerve Pathway Placement
- Along peripheral nerve distribution
- Over nerve roots and plexuses
- Creating circuits with positive/negative pairs

#### Systemic Placement
- Organ-specific locations
- Lymphatic drainage pathways
- Acupuncture or trigger points

#### Combination Protocols
- Multiple disc types for complex conditions
- Strategic placement for synergistic effects
- Addressing multiple systems simultaneously

## Contraindications

### Absolute Contraindications
- Pacemakers or implanted electrical devices
- Pregnancy (first trimester)
- Active cancer in treatment area
- Skin infections or open wounds at application site

### Relative Contraindications
- Severe cardiovascular conditions (requires medical clearance)
- Epilepsy (requires caution and monitoring)
- Pregnancy (second and third trimesters - requires caution)
- Skin sensitivities or allergies

## Clinical Application Guidelines

### Treatment Session
1. **Assessment**: 15-20 minutes comprehensive evaluation
2. **Disc Selection**: Choose appropriate disc types
3. **Placement**: Strategic positioning based on condition
4. **Education**: Patient instruction on disc care and expectations
5. **Follow-up Planning**: Schedule reassessment

### Patient Instructions
- Keep discs dry (shower with caution or remove)
- Wear continuously for prescribed duration (typically 72 hours)
- Monitor for skin reactions (rare)
- Report changes in symptoms
- Return for disc changes as scheduled

### Treatment Frequency
- **Acute**: Every 2-3 days initially
- **Chronic**: Every 3-5 days
- **Maintenance**: Weekly or as needed
- **Duration**: Varies by condition, typically 4-12 weeks

- May feel warmth or tingling initially (normal response)
- Pain reduction may be immediate or gradual
- Some conditions require multiple applications
- Discs are waterproof but should be protected during showering

### Self-Care
- Maintain hydration
- Continue prescribed exercises
- Monitor symptoms and report changes
- Protect discs from excessive moisture
- Follow activity recommendations
